FAQ

Is this beginner friendly?

Yes. A complete beginner can start on day one: every principle, lift and nutrition concept is explained from zero. Intermediate and advanced lifters use the same system to break long plateaus.

Can I build muscle while losing fat?

Yes, especially as a beginner, an intermediate, or when returning after a break. Both the Blueprint and 1:1 coaching use body recomposition as the default approach.

How fast will I see results?

Most people see visible changes within 4–6 weeks of consistent execution. Undeniable physique change usually lands between months 3 and 6, and from there every month compounds.

Do I need supplements?

No. Supplements are optional and only recommended when they genuinely help. Real food, sleep and consistency do 95% of the work.

Can I train from home?

Yes. The principles work in a gym, a home gym or with minimal equipment. In 1:1 coaching your plan is built around the equipment you actually have.

Do you coach internationally?

Yes. All coaching is online, with clients across Europe, the UK, the US, Canada, Australia and the Middle East.

Night shifts or a busy schedule?

No problem. Plans are built around your real life: your sleep, your shifts, your travel. Several of the best transformations happened during night-shift rotations.

How do I receive the Blueprint?

Instantly. As soon as your payment goes through you get access to the full system, with lifetime access and every future update included.

Blueprint or 1:1 coaching?

The Blueprint is the complete system, ideal if you want to execute on your own. 1:1 coaching is fully personalised, hands-on guidance from me with weekly adjustments and direct support.
